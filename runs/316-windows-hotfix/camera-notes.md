# 316 camera scroll candidate

The local 3D EDG scroll check allowed only the contracted 2D viewport bounds. This candidate extends the horizontal and top scroll limits by approximately one third of the logical viewport divided by zoom, snapped to the hex grid. The existing floor-based bottom stop remains in use. EDG data, forced positioning, map clipping and movement geometry are unchanged.

`camera-left.jpg` compares the old and candidate left stops in copies of the owner's ARCAVES.SAV e2, with hero tile 21125, initial center tile 22307, zoom 1.28 and logical 1720 x 720. The test uses a background window and a preview hero, not the owner's borderless window. The left stop moved from tile 19930 to 18743, or 416 logical pixels. The right limit moved by the same amount; the top moved 144 pixels; the original candidate kept the straight bottom stop at tile 24916. At logical 960 x 540 the left limit moved 224 pixels. Sequential left-then-up and left-then-down probes reached stable corners; the latter still used the floor stop.

Not established: the original inability to move from center tile 22307 in the owner's borderless session, the actual door click at the new position, 2D or world-map behavior in a successful runtime comparison, and further bottom coverage on other maps. Classic 2D scroll probes were unstable both before and after and do not validate equivalence. These gaps remain open; this candidate is not a release approval.

Round 2: the stop can move inward when zoom increases or the player returns to 2D. A step from outside is admitted only when it decreases at least one out-of-range component and increases none. At zoom 1.0 the e2 left stop was tile 18347; after zoom 1.28, scrolling right recovered to tile 25276. After switching from the 3D left stop tile 18743 to 2D, scrolling right reached the legacy tile 24089. These were background, instrumented tests. The authored bottom limit on this fixture is y=2064; its previous floor-based stop was y=2184 and the candidate reached y=2208, 24 pixels beyond that prior effective stop. The bottom floor-extension classification remains based on the original authored limit.

Manual scroll on local maps without an active EDG zone uses the floor footprint, with the same viewport-scaled extra travel. On the vanilla Klamath entrance at 1920x1080 (logical 960x540), 250 left steps from tile 25075 reached 23689 on baseline and 12799 on the candidate. On the New Reno entrance at 3440x1440 (logical 1720x720), zoom 1.28, the same probe from tile 24305 reached 22919 on baseline and 14999 on the candidate. Returning to 2D after passing the old limit returns the view to the hero so legacy scrolling works. Returning after a short pan preserves the nearby camera position. The no-floor fallback is covered by code-level bounds checks; a runtime map without floor bounds was not exercised.

### Lower corner correction

The added range initially applied its horizontal limits to the existing authored-bottom clip test. At ARCAVES.MAP e2 center 21753 (EDG offsets x=5152 and y=2208), the original horizontal boundary is x=4736. The scroll candidate enabled the existing floor extension there, changing bottom plane offset from 20.9445038 to 25.4911747. `eb25945b8` uses the original EDG limits for the clip test and keeps the expanded manual scroll bounds.

Live captures with hero 21125, center 21753, 3440x1440 at 2x, zoom 1.28 and turn 0 used `6f5b82bf3`, `d40acf3fc` and `eb25945b8`. The expanded camera initially differed from main in 294,301 pixels, mostly in the bottom band. The corrected frame has no differing pixels. The bottom row in [the camera comparison](camera-left.jpg) shows that correction. An instrumented scroll down from center 18743 still reached 21753. At center 25317 inside the old sideways bounds the existing bottom floor extension still applies. At +15 degrees the clip planes matched clean main and the differences were confined to a small region above the boundary. Clicks and full borderless acceptance remain open.

The copied owner save was also loaded as `ARCAVES.SAV` e2. Actual game scrolling from 22307 reached 18743 with hero 21125 and zoom 1.28 at 3440x1440 and 2x. Live baseline and fixed frames on the saved map at 21753 share the authored planes. The hero and egg are on different animation frames, so the full PNGs differ. These off-screen runs match the viewport dimensions but do not exercise the owner's desktop-fullscreen mode.

On vanilla KLADWTWN e0, placing the hero on the existing world-map exit at tile 23454 opened the same 2D map on `6f5b82bf3` and `eb25945b8`. The captured windows matched pixel for pixel. Walking to the exit and returning from the world map were not verified. The EDG toggle test recovered from center 18743 to 24089 using normal scroll steps after switching to 2D.

The projected-floor fallback is used only for the loaded local scene when its overlay is active. The two cached elevations with empty floors are ENCFITE e1 and e2. The attempted e1 load found no walkable tile and fell back to e0, so it does not prove what happens when the projection is temporarily unavailable on a playable map.
