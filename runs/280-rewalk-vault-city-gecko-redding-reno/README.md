## Notes

- No new geometry defects found in this walk (29 maps, 118 spots, game camera and +45 fly turn each; bhrndmtn, newr1a, newr2a had no spot files, so their views were generated per building with tools/gallery_spots.py).
- Chain-link fence sections (geckjunk pens, vctycocl outer wall, modinn yard in 279) still render as dark mesh with bright specks: known find of 191, cut as task 276 (draft).
- New Reno is night-dark by design; Broken Hills mine levels, Redding tunnels and the Gecko/Redding cave interiors are dark in the player view. Geometry was judged where visible; lighting levels are out of scope.
- Fly-view cells fully covered by a roof or wall (a few New Reno and tunnel spots) were judged from the game-camera view of the same spot; the tour distance 8 sits low and close, interiors get blocked (harness limit, not a defect).
