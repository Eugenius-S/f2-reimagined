# Temple entrance floor ownership

This comparison shows the same executable, camera, and 09:00 sunlight,
first with the optional ownership mask absent and then present. The hero's
idle animation differs. The stairs and the two lower tower sections are part
of the original floor art; the mask connects them to the same sunlight verdict
as the scenery pieces above. The unpainted foreground remains on the regular
floor-lighting path. A different map or a changed tile/object guard skips the
mask. The cache exporter generates it from checked-in outlines, not from a
copy of the game's art.
