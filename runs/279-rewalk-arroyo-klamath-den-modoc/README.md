## Notes

- No new geometry defects found in this walk. Every suspicious dark cell was re-checked at full resolution against the classic 2D view at the same tile; the 3D view matched (including the upper-floor look of denbus2 e1, gstcav2 e1 and denbus1 e1/e2, which shows a bare floor strip over black in the original too).
- arvill2 (all 6 generated stops): skip. The map is not in the worldmap registry, so the dev tour refuses to enter it (requestMapChange log: not in the worldmap registry); a scripted save or a direct start on that map is a separate fixture job.
- The Den chain-link fences (denbus1 t17292 view and the yard fences) still render as dark mesh with bright specks: known find 1 of 191, cut as task 276 (draft).
- Caves and night-dark elevations (arcaves e1/e2, gstcav1/2, modshit, modwell, klaratcv, klatoxcv) are dark in the player view; geometry was judged where visible. Lighting levels are out of scope here.
- Flat 2D cliffs and boundary walls show edge-on or not at all in the turned fly view (arbridge t20686 gorge, modgard and klacanyn map edges, klatrap pond): known flat-art class, visible only in the fly view.
- kladwtwn fly views past the map edge show a white or tan void (cosmetic, out-of-map area only).
- Tooling note for the next walk: -TurnDegrees and -FlyTurn do not reach the camera in background runs; the working way to shoot a turned view is the tour stop suffix (map:elevation:tile:-1:45:8, the same syntax tools/tour/tour.py uses).
